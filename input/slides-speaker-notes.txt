TRACK 01 · 4 OF 9 TABLES
Welcome journey for people arriving in Milan
For whom. New residents, international students, people who don't speak Italian.
The job. Tax code, residency, housing, waste tax, transport: what to do, in which order, with which City source.
Build on. The City's existing welcome emails and the YesMilano student path.
Claude at work
An agent that talks or chats in the newcomer's language, reasons over City rules and builds their personal checklist.
Watch out
Without identity data it can become a smarter FAQ. Show what data the next version would need.
Filone raccomandato nel seed deck: unisce onboarding, proattività e accesso unico. Persona pilota concreta. V1 solo conoscenza pubblica. Punto debole: senza dati del cittadino il "proattivo" resta una promessa.
7

TRACK 02 · 2 OF 9 TABLES
One procedure, assisted from start to submission
For whom. Citizens and professionals filing with the City.
The job. Pick one frequent procedure (a SCIA, a parking permit, a certificate) and take it apart: fields, attachments, typical errors, reasons for rejection.
Measure it. Time and errors with and without the assistant.
Claude at work
Reads documents and photos, asks only what is missing, pre-fills and checks before submission.
Watch out
A wrong pre-fill falls on the citizen. Claude fills in, the person checks and signs.
Impatto misurabile e caso ripetibile: se funziona su una pratica si estende alle altre. Le schede citavano oltre 12 mila SCIA ferme. Nessun dato personale reale: casi anonimi o inventati.
8

TRACK 03 · 2 OF 9 TABLES
From inside City Hall: the people at the counter
For whom. City staff at service desks and back offices, and through them, every citizen they serve.
The job. Repeated questions, manual hand-offs, requests that land in the wrong office.
Why bother. The tables named inertia, culture and missing skills as the biggest brakes. This track works on those directly.
Claude at work
Triages incoming requests, routes them, drafts answers with sources for the officer to approve.
Watch out
The benefit to citizens is indirect. Make it visible in your demo.
Crea i promotori interni che servono per portare avanti gli altri filoni. Se in sala ci sono dipendenti del Comune, sono i domain expert ideali per questi team.

TODAY'S MAIN RULE
Claude builds it, and Claude runs it
BUILT WITH CLAUDE
Claude writes your app
With Claude Code, Cowork or Claude Design you can prototype in hours. Every team will do this, so it won't set you apart.
RUNS ON CLAUDE
Claude does the work, every time
When a citizen or an officer uses your solution, Claude reads the request, applies the City's rules and fills in or routes what it can. It runs through the API, as an agent or behind an MCP server.
Slide chiave. Il messaggio: non vogliamo una web app costruita con Claude e poi statica. Vogliamo soluzioni in cui l'AI è il motore. Per questo c'è un criterio di giudizio dedicato e un requisito nel README. I crediti API servono esattamente a questo.

TIPS AND TRICKS
Three ways to get more out of Claude today
Brainstorm in an HTML artifact
Ask Claude for an interactive page that asks you questions one at a time and draws your answers. Use it for personas and for how the service could evolve. Ask it to be very visual.
Use Claude Design twice
First to design the user experience from your user stories. Then again at the end, to build the pitch deck, with video if you have it.
Let Claude make your demo video
With Opus 5.5 in Claude Desktop or Claude Code, point it at your repo and ask for a two-minute video. Add the docs that explain what you built, not just the code.
Prompt di esempio per il brainstorming: "Crea una pagina HTML interattiva che ci intervisti una domanda alla volta per definire le personas di questo servizio. Man mano che rispondiamo, disegna le personas come schede visive." Per il video: più contesto c'è nel repo (README, user stories, note sulle scelte), migliore è il risultato.
